How the 52-week challenge works

In week 1 you save 1, in week 2 you save 2, and so on until week 52, when you save 52. By the end of the year you've saved 1,378 – without ever feeling a big hit. Raise the weekly step to 2 or 5 to save 2,756 or 6,890.

The reverse version starts with the biggest amount, while you're most motivated, and ends with the smallest – handy because December is usually expensive.
