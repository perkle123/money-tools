Debt snowball vs debt avalanche

With both methods you pay the minimum on every debt and put all extra money on one target debt. When it's paid off, its whole payment rolls into the next debt – so your payment grows like a snowball.

Snowball targets the smallest balance first. You get quick wins, which keeps you motivated. Avalanche targets the highest interest rate first, which is mathematically cheapest. The calculator above shows you the difference for your own debts.

How accurate is this?

Interest is calculated monthly (APR / 12). Real lenders may calculate daily interest or add fees, so treat the result as a close estimate.
