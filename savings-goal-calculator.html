Tips to reach your savings goal faster

  • Automate it: set up an automatic transfer to a separate savings account on payday.
  • Name the account after the goal ("Japan 2027") – it's much harder to dip into.
  • Round up: save every raise, refund and bonus before you get used to spending it.
  • Start with an emergency fund of 1–3 months of expenses so surprises don't end up on a credit card.
