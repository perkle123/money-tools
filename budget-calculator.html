What is the 50/30/20 rule?

The 50/30/20 rule splits your income after tax into three buckets: 50% for needs (housing, utilities, groceries, transport, insurance and minimum loan payments), 30% for wants (eating out, hobbies, streaming, shopping, holidays) and 20% for savings and debt payoff (emergency fund, savings goals, investing and extra payments on debt).

What if my needs are more than 50%?

That's common when rent is high. Use a 60/20/20 split instead and look at your biggest fixed costs one by one – switching electricity, phone or insurance providers often frees up money quickly.

Where do loan payments go?

Minimum payments count as needs. Anything extra you pay to get out of debt faster belongs in the savings & debt bucket. Try the debt payoff calculator to see how much faster you'd be debt-free.
